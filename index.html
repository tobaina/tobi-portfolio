You do not need the diagnostic to start. It earns its CA$400 when the problem is a tangled operation nobody has mapped yet, and it comes straight off the build price if you go ahead. A website, a landing page or a small tool starts with a brief instead. What moves a quote is the number of screens, how much of the content already exists, how many systems it has to talk to, and whether anyone has to be able to edit it afterwards.

How payment works. Under CA$5,000: half to book the dates, half on handover. Above that: 40% to book, 40% at an agreed midpoint, 20% on handover. Anything outside the written scope is quoted before it is built, never after.

First two system builds: free. The next two first-phase builds cost nothing. You cover only the third-party services the system runs on, and those accounts stay in your name. In exchange I write the work up as a case study, with your permission and with anything private removed. After those two, list price.
